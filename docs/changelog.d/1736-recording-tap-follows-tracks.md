- **Recording: the master no longer goes silent for participants whose track was re-created mid-call (#1736).** The browser recording tap mixed the page's audio elements once at start, so anyone whose remote track was re-negotiated later — unmute after a mute, a rejoin, a reconnect — was recorded as digital silence for the rest of the meeting (a Jitsi daily lost three of five voices for 24 of 55 minutes while the live transcript had them all). The mix now follows the page: elements that appear, and elements whose `srcObject` is swapped, join the recording within two seconds; nothing is connected twice. Affects every browser-recorded platform (Google Meet, Teams, Jitsi, Telemost). See [Recordings](/how-to/recordings).
